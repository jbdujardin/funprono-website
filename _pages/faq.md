# FAQ

**Q: Until when can I register a prediction?**

**A:** You have up to 5 minutes before the start of the match to register your prediction

**Q: How do I invite a friend to join my league?**

**A:** To invite a friend to join your league, simply provide them with your league's invitation code.
Your friend will then simply enter this code to join your league.

**Q: How to earn more points?**

**A:** You can double the number of points earned by finding the exact score of a match.

**Q: How can I delete my account?**

**A:** You can delete your account by going to the app settings, there is a red button at the bottom of the screen to delete your account. 
No data concerning you will be kept.
You can also send us an email at contact@funprono.com and we will delete your account for you.

**Q: Can I request deletion of my data without deleting my account?**

**A:** You can send us an email at contact@funprono.com to ask us to delete your data or only certain data concerning you.
